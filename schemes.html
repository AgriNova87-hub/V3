Government Agri Schemes

Explore agriculture subsidies, credit support, and crop insurance programs across India.

PM-KISAN

Provides eligible farmers with direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments.

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Comprehensive crop insurance scheme protecting farmers against losses caused by natural disasters, pests, and diseases.

PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana

Promotes micro-irrigation systems, efficient water usage, and farm water management infrastructure.

Soil Health Card

Provides soil nutrient reports and customized fertilizer dosage recommendations to maximize productivity.

e-NAM Platform

Pan-India electronic trading portal connecting existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Delivers timely short-term credit support for cultivation, equipment purchase, and post-harvest maintenance at concessional interest rates.
