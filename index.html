About AgriNova

AgriNova bridges hardware innovation with modern Web IoT technology. Connected to ESP32 microcontrollers, moisture sensors, and temperature modules, AgriNova prevents over-irrigation, conserves groundwater, and delivers actionable insights straight to your phone or desktop.
